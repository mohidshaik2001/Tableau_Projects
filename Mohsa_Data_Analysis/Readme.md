# Mohsa Data Analysis Project

## 📊 Overview

This Tableau project presents a suite of interactive dashboards analyzing **sales performance**, **customer behavior**, and **delivery operations**. Designed for business leaders and analysts, it helps uncover trends, diagnose inefficiencies, and identify growth opportunities using clean visual storytelling.

## 📈 Dashboards Overview

### 🛒 **Sales Dashboard**
**Focus**: Track overall revenue, profitability, product trends, and sales distribution.

**Key Features**:
- Monthly and quarterly trend lines for Sales and Profit
- KPI cards for quick reference (Sales, Profit, Quantity, Orders)
- Breakdown by Product Category, Sub-category, and Region
- Profit vs. Sales scatter plots for margin analysis

**Detailed Insights**:
- High-performing categories consistently drive over 60% of total revenue.
- Certain low-revenue sub-categories show high profit margins — ideal for upselling or cross-promotion.
- Sales dip observed in Q2 due to inventory cycle — potential area for stock-level optimization.
- Top 10 products contribute disproportionately to total profit.

---

### 👤 **Customer Dashboard**
**Focus**: Explore customer base behavior and identify high-value segments.

**Key Features**:
- Unique customer count and average orders per customer
- New vs. repeat customer distribution
- Region-wise customer clustering
- Heatmaps showing order frequency

**Detailed Insights**:
- 30% of customers are repeat buyers, contributing nearly 50% of revenue.
- Urban zones account for the highest order frequency, especially Tier 1 cities.
- Most loyal customers (top 10%) generate 3x more orders than the average customer.
- Orders per customer show strong correlation with regional campaigns.

---

### 🚚 **Delivery Dashboard**
**Focus**: Analyze delivery timeliness, operational cost, and bottlenecks.

**Key Features**:
- KPIs: Avg. Delivery Cost, Time, and Fulfillment Rate
- City-level breakdown of deliveries
- Delivery status chart: Delivered, Delayed, Returned
- Trend line for average delivery time

**Detailed Insights**:
- Avg. delivery time has increased by 1.5 days in the last two quarters — largely in the southern region.
- 15% of total delivery costs come from just 3 cities — likely due to distance or third-party logistics.
- Returned items spike during discount campaigns — return policy optimization needed.
- Timely deliveries have a direct correlation with higher customer satisfaction and repeat rates.

## 🛠 Tools Used

- **Tableau Desktop** (2021.1 or higher)
- Embedded `.hyper` data extract
- Custom-calculated fields, dynamic filters, and visual KPIs
- Heatmaps, trend lines, and scatter plots for exploratory analysis

## 🔍 How to Use

1. Download the `.twbx` file from the link above.
2. Open with Tableau Desktop.
3. Use interactive filters (e.g., region, category, time) to drill down.
4. Optional: Replace the data source using the files in the shared data folder.
